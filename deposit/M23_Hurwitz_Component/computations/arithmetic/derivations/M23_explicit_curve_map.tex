\section*{2. The $M_{23}$ component and its equation}
Let $X_{M_{23}}$ be the unique irreducible component of

\[

\begin{theorem}[Exact curve and map]
The ordered Hurwitz curve is the normalization $C$ of the explicitly defined
saturated component above.  Its canonical base-coordinate map is
\[
 \boxed{\beta:C\longrightarrow\Pone_\lambda,\qquad
        \beta(\lambda,\mathbf z)=[\lambda:1].}
\]
The map has degree $980$ and the source has genus $119$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The two polynomial identities encode the four required ramification fibers,
and saturation restricts to smooth four-branch-point covers.  The certified
Nielsen computation identifies one transitive component containing exactly
$980$ ordered cover classes above a generic value of $\lambda$.  Therefore
projection to $\lambda$ has degree $980$.  Its peripheral cycle counts are
$368,188,188$, hence its indices are $612,792,792$.  Riemann--Hurwitz gives
\[
 2g(C)-2=-2\cdot980+612+792+792=236,
\]
so $g(C)=119$.
\end{proof}

A plane eliminant is obtained without introducing a $980$-dimensional source
algebra.  Choose a primitive element $u\in\Q(X_{M_{23}})$ over $\Q(\lambda)$
and define

\[

\[
